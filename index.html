BEFORE SHARING · AFTER SHARING

Preparing a photo and checking it later are two different jobs.

A social app, messenger, editor or screenshot can leave you with a file that is no longer identical to the copy you first prepared. SIGNA is built around that reality: prepare a sharing copy before it leaves your phone, then inspect the file you have later instead of expecting one watermark to answer every question.

01

Prepare

Choose a visible watermark when attribution should be obvious, or use the invisible SIGNA signal when you do not want a mark across the image.

02

Share

Use the places you already share photos. Resizing, cropping, recompression or screenshots may change the file along the way.

03

Inspect

When you need to look again, review the SIGNA signal, available metadata and image-state information on the file in front of you.

01 · PROTECT

When attribution should be visible,
make it look intentional.

Add text or a logo, then tune font, color, size, opacity and position so the mark belongs on the photo rather than feeling pasted on. Process up to 12 photos at once, adjust placement per photo, and save designs you reuse as presets. In the visible-watermark flow, SIGNA also adds the invisible SIGNA signal automatically.

Text & logo · up to 12 photos · per-photo placement · presets · downloadable font packs for 8 languages · invisible SIGNA signal added in the same flow

02 · PROTECT

When a watermark would cover the photo,
keep the image visually clean.

Add the invisible SIGNA signal without placing visible text or a logo across the image. Choose Original when you are preparing a high-quality reference copy or SNS when the file is intended for social sharing, and process up to 9 photos at once. Later signal observability depends on what happens to the image after that point.

Original and SNS preparation · up to 9 photos · optional personal key and local record · resizing, cropping, recompression and screenshots can change later signal observability

DOCUMENT IMAGES · INVISIBLE SIGNAL

When a document becomes a JPG or PNG, you may want a layer that does not cover the page.

PDF and office documents can offer passwords, permissions or other document-level controls. A document shared as a JPG or PNG is different. If a visible watermark would get in the way of the text, SIGNA can add an invisible SIGNA signal to a supported document image without placing a visible mark over the page. Later, you can inspect that image in SIGNA to see whether the signal is observable.

Useful for estimates, confirmations and other business materials shared as images · keeps the page readable without a visible mark over the content · later image processing can affect signal detectability
