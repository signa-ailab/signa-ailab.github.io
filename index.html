WHY SIGNA · PROTECT → INSPECT → REPORT

Your photo deserves more than a mark in the corner.

A visible watermark can show your name or brand, but it changes the photo and can be cropped away. An invisible signal keeps the image visually clean, but later processing can weaken it. SIGNA uses both as complementary layers, then lets you inspect the image and signal information when you need it. It is a practical pre-share workflow for photographers, creators, sellers and anyone handling images they care about — with selected-photo processing kept on your phone.

02 · PROTECT

A photo you keep and a photo you post
do not need the same treatment.

Choose Original for files you plan to keep or SNS for photos intended for social sharing, and process up to 10 at a time. You can also register a personal authentication key and link its reference to the SIGNA signal. The key is not sent to a server; it stays in local storage on your phone. Later, SIGNA Inspect can compare the signal with local key records on that phone. For JPEG images, SIGNA uses a DCT-based frequency-domain method.

DOCUMENT IMAGES · INVISIBLE SIGNAL

When a document becomes a JPG or PNG, you may want a layer that does not cover the page.

PDF and office documents can offer passwords, permissions or other document-level controls. A document shared as a JPG or PNG is different. If a visible watermark would get in the way of the text, SIGNA can add an invisible SIGNA signal to a supported document image without placing a visible mark over the page. Later, you can inspect that image in SIGNA to see whether the signal is observable.

Useful for document images such as estimates, confirmations and other business materials · not file encryption or access control · does not prove authenticity, ownership, who exchanged the document, or its transmission history · later processing can affect signal detectability
