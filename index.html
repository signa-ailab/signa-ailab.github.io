Local-first by design

Keep the main image workflow close to your device.

SIGNA is designed around on-device processing for its main image preparation and analysis functions. That means you can work with personal photos without making a remote AI upload part of the core workflow.

What SIGNA does not claim

Invisible signals can be affected by resizing, cropping, recompression, screenshots, and platform processing. SIGNA does not promise universal detection or survival across every service and transformation.
