Prepare. Review.
Keep a record.

Add a watermark or SIGNA signal before sharing, review the image later, and organize what you find in a report.

01 · PROTECT

Make your watermark
fit the photo.

Add text or logo watermarks and adjust their placement and appearance. When you apply a visible watermark, SIGNA also adds an invisible SIGNA signal.

Text & logo watermarks · placement and styling · invisible SIGNA signal added automatically
02 · PROTECT

Add an invisible
SIGNA signal.

Add an invisible SIGNA signal without placing a visible mark on the photo. For JPEG images, SIGNA’s current signal path uses a DCT-based frequency-domain method.

Invisible signals may be affected by resizing, cropping, recompression, screenshots, and platform processing.
03 · INSPECT

Review the image
with more context.

Review reference information about the image state alongside SIGNA signal information. These results do not determine whether an image was edited, composited, or AI-generated.

Key image preparation and analysis features are designed for on-device processing.
04 · REPORT

Bring the details together
in one report.

Pro Evidence Report brings together the image information reviewed in SIGNA, along with relevant limitations, in a report you can save or export.

  • Reference information read from the file, including camera and EXIF data
  • Image-state and processing-related reference signals
  • SIGNA signal-related information and limitations
LOCAL-FIRST

Built around
on-device processing.

SIGNA is designed to handle key image preparation and analysis functions on your device, without requiring an upload to an external AI service as part of those core functions.
