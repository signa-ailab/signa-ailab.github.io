Prepare. Review.
Keep a record.

SIGNA connects the workflow from preparing a photo before sharing to reviewing useful image information later and organizing what you observed into a report.

01 · PROTECT

Visible watermarks,
your way.

Add text or image-logo watermarks and adjust their placement and appearance. In the visible-watermark flow, SIGNA also records an invisible SIGNA signal.

Text & logo watermarks · placement and styling · SIGNA signal in supported workflows
02 · PROTECT

An invisible
SIGNA signal.

Apply an invisible SIGNA signal while keeping the visual appearance of the photo. The current JPEG signal path uses a DCT-based frequency-domain approach.

Invisible signals may be affected by resizing, cropping, recompression, screenshots, and platform processing.
03 · INSPECT

When you need to
look at an image again.

Review image-state clues and SIGNA signal-related information. The results are reference information and do not determine whether an image was edited, composited, or AI-generated.

The main image preparation and analysis functions are designed around on-device processing.
04 · REPORT

Organize what you
observed in a report.

Pro Evidence Report organizes the image information you reviewed and relevant limitations into a detailed report that can be saved or exported.

  • Reference information read from the file, including camera and EXIF data
  • Image-state and processing-related reference signals
  • SIGNA signal-related information and limitations
LOCAL-FIRST BY DESIGN

Keep the core image workflow
on your device.

SIGNA is designed around on-device processing for its main image preparation and analysis functions, without making an upload to an external AI service a required core step.
