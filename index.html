The job doesn’t always end when you send the file

Say you’re sending a client a set of finished photos and an estimate. The photos need your name. The estimate needs every line to stay readable. Here’s how to handle both.

01

Get the files ready

Give the photos a visible watermark. On the estimate image, add an invisible SIGNA signal.

02

Send them the way you usually do

Use your regular messaging or email app. Keep the copy you saved with the signal in it.

03

Open the file when it comes up again

Look for the signal and check the file details in SIGNA Inspect, using your saved copy or the file you received.

01 · WATERMARK

A watermark that belongs in the shot

Give a whole delivery the same look. Watermark up to 12 photos at once, then move the mark on the shots that need it. Set the size, opacity, and placement of your name or logo, choose a font and color for text, and save the design for the next job.

Portrait and landscape · Up to 12 photos · Placement per photo · Saved designs · Extra fonts with Pro
Choose a watermark for your photo →
02 · SIGNA SIGNAL

Keep the price and fine print in view

When there’s no good place for a logo, add the invisible SIGNA signal on its own. You can also use it alongside a visible watermark. Choose Original for a copy you’re keeping or SNS for a copy you’re sharing, and work through up to nine images at a time.

Original and SNS modes · Up to 9 photos · Optional key · Signal records on your device
Using an invisible SIGNA signal →
DOCUMENT IMAGES

Sending an estimate as a JPG or PNG?

Keep the original document, then add a SIGNA signal to the image you’re sending. The numbers and stamps stay easy to read. If the image comes back to you, look for the signal in SIGNA Inspect. Compression and screenshots can make it harder to read, so keep the copy you saved.

Quotes and confirmations · JPG or PNG · Invisible SIGNA signal
Add a signal to a document image →
03 · IMAGE ANALYSIS

See what came back to you

Open your saved copy or an image a client sent back. Look for the SIGNA signal and any matching record stored on your phone. Then check the file details, EXIF data, and JPEG compression settings, or save an analysis map to your gallery.

SIGNA signal · File details and EXIF · Image condition
