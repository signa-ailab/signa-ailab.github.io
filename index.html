PROTECT → INSPECT → REPORT

Prepare photos before you share.
Check and document them later.

SIGNA goes beyond placing a watermark on a photo. Prepare what you plan to publish, add an invisible SIGNA signal alongside the visible mark, then review image information and SIGNA signals when you need them. Save presets, process up to 10 photos at once, keep results, and export a report when a structured record is useful.

01 · PROTECT

Make the watermark yours.
Fine-tune each photo when needed.

Add text or a logo, then adjust font, color, size, opacity and position. Process up to 10 photos at once while keeping per-photo placement control, and save designs you reuse as presets. Downloadable font packs add typography options for 8 languages. Applying a visible watermark also adds an invisible SIGNA signal automatically.

Text & logo · up to 10 photos · per-photo placement · presets · downloadable font packs for 8 languages · invisible SIGNA signal added automatically
02 · PROTECT

A photo you keep and a photo you post
do not need the same treatment.

Choose Original for files you plan to keep or SNS for photos intended for social sharing, and process up to 10 at a time. You can also register a personal authentication key and link its reference to the SIGNA signal. The key is not sent to a server; it stays in local storage on your phone. Later, SIGNA Inspect can compare the signal with local key records on that phone. For JPEG images, SIGNA uses a DCT-based frequency-domain method.

Original for files you keep · SNS for social sharing · up to 10 photos · personal authentication key · key stays on the phone · local comparison in SIGNA Inspect · resizing, cropping, recompression and screenshots can affect detectability
03 · INSPECT

Useful image details,
all in one place.

Review resolution, dimensions, file size, compression and file condition, available metadata, Content Credentials presentation, SIGNA signal information, and indicators that may relate to editing or other changes — without having to dig through specialist tools.

SIGNA presents technical clues and reference information. It does not, by itself, prove ownership, legal status, AI generation, or a complete edit history.
04 · REPORT

Save what you find.
Export it when you need a record.

Keep analyzed results on your phone and bring the relevant information together in a Pro Evidence Report you can save or export.

  • Camera, EXIF, metadata and available Content Credentials information
  • Image-state, compression and processing-related information
  • SIGNA signal information, saved results and relevant technical limitations
