PROTECT → INSPECT → REPORT

More than a watermark.
A workflow for photos you share.

Prepare photos before publishing, add visible and invisible layers, inspect SIGNA signals and image information later, then save or export what you find. Presets, batch processing, saved results and reports keep the workflow practical for everyday use.

01 · PROTECT

Watermarks built
for real workflows.

Add text or logo watermarks and fine-tune font, color, size, opacity and position. Process up to 10 images at once, adjust placement per image, save favorite designs as presets, and expand typography with downloadable font packs for 8 languages. Applying a visible watermark also adds an invisible SIGNA signal automatically.

Text & logo · up to 10 images · per-image placement · presets · 8-language downloadable font packs · invisible SIGNA signal added automatically
02 · PROTECT

Add a second layer
you don’t see.

Add an invisible SIGNA signal without placing a visible mark on the photo. Choose Original or SNS depending on how you plan to save or share the image, and process up to 10 images at a time. For JPEG images, SIGNA uses a DCT-based frequency-domain method.

Original / SNS modes · up to 10 images · signal inspection later · platform resizing, cropping, recompression or screenshots can affect detectability
03 · INSPECT

See more of what
the image can tell you.

Review resolution, dimensions, file size, compression and file condition, available metadata, Content Credentials presentation, SIGNA signal information, and indicators that may relate to editing or other changes — in a form designed to be readable without specialist tools.

SIGNA presents technical clues and reference information; it does not by itself prove ownership, legal status, AI generation, or a complete edit history.
04 · REPORT

Keep the details.
Not just the moment.

Save analyzed results locally and bring the information together in a Pro Evidence Report you can keep or export when you need a structured record.

  • Camera, EXIF, metadata and available Content Credentials information
  • Image-state, compression and processing-related information
  • SIGNA signal information, saved results and relevant technical limitations
LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY

Your photos stay
with you.

Watermarking, SIGNA signal inspection and image analysis are processed on your device. SIGNA does not upload your photos to its own servers for watermarking or image analysis. Your personal photos do not need to become server-side analysis material just to use SIGNA’s core photo tools.

Prepare smarter. Share with more control.

Visible watermark · invisible SIGNA signal · image analysis · local-first privacy
