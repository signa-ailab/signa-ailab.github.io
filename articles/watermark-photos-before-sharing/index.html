WATERMARK BEFORE SHARING

How to Watermark Photos Before Posting or Sharing Them

Published September 18, 2026 · SIGNA AI Lab

Before you post: choose the right watermark

A visible text or logo mark tells viewers something immediately. An invisible signal serves a different machine-readable role. Before sharing, decide what you want the mark to do, keep the visible layer readable without overwhelming the photo, and remember that later compression, resizing, cropping or screenshots can change what remains observable.

Text, logo, or invisible signal?

Use text when a name or handle should stay visible. Use a logo when consistent branding matters. An invisible signal adds a separate layer without covering the image, giving you another technical clue to check later. Cropping, resizing, recompression or screenshots can affect whether that signal remains observable.

Invisible watermark: how it works and what can break it

A practical pre-share workflow

Keep an original copy. Prepare a separate sharing copy when appropriate. Check visible privacy details and available metadata, add the layers you need, then review the exported file before posting. For several photos, a batch workflow can keep placement and settings consistent.

Check photo privacy before sharing

What happens after you share

Social and messaging services can recompress, resize, crop or otherwise process an image. A watermark that is visible before upload can look different afterward, and an invisible signal may become harder to recover. Treat robustness as conditional on the actual transformation.
