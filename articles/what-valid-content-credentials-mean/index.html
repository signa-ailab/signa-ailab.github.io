Photo provenance · C2PA verification

What does a valid Content Credential actually tell you about a photo?

A photo of a flooded street goes around with the caption “this morning.” Its Content Credential checks out. The record can tell you about the image file and how it was made. To check the caption, you still need the original post or other reporting from the scene.

November 13, 2026 · SIGNA AI Lab

Start with the signer

Open the details for the actual file in a tool that can validate Content Credentials. C2PA calls the signed record a manifest. It can contain statements about the camera, editing software or changes made to the image. If an editor signed the record at export, look at what it says it did and whether it includes any earlier history. The signer shown may be the software or service that created the record.

Does the record match the copy you received?

A verifier also checks the binding between the record and the file. It compares a stored hash with one calculated from the parts of the file covered by the binding. Re-exporting a photo can change those parts even when the picture looks the same. If the file match fails, ask for the file before it was resized or re-saved, then check that copy.

A quick guide to the three manifest states

  • Well-Formed means the record meets the specification’s structural and content requirements.
  • Valid means the record also passes checks such as signature validation and has stayed intact since signing.
  • Trusted means the valid record’s signing credential also meets the verifier’s trust criteria.

Those are terms in the C2PA specification. A site may show a shorter label. Open the full result to see which checks actually passed and what was recorded.

When there’s nothing to open

Some cameras and apps never attach a record. A screenshot or shared copy can also lose it. The absence of a credential tells you little about whether AI was used. When the stakes are higher than a quick repost, trace the image back to its first known publication and compare the date, location and caption with other sources.
